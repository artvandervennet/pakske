# Code of conduct

Pakske's issues, pull requests and discussions are for working on Pakske together. Everyone is welcome to take part, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume the other person means well.
- Criticise the code or the idea, not the person.
- Say what you don't understand. Nobody has to know everything.
- Accept a "no" on a proposal, and give your reasons when you say no to someone else's.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, mockery or personal attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including unwelcome sexual attention.
- Remarks that put people down for who they are.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Keeping on at someone who asked you to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here is a problem for you or for someone else, mail <me@vandervennet.art>. Say what happened and where; a link helps. Your report stays between you and the maintainer.

## What happens next

The maintainer reads every report and decides what to do: nothing, a word in private, removing a comment, or blocking someone from the repository, for a while or for good. The maintainer may also remove comments, commits and other contributions that go against this document without a report.

This applies in this repository and wherever someone speaks for the project.
